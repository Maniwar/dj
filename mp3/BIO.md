# Artist bio — SYSTEM OVERLOAD

Three lengths, because every platform asks for a different one. Paste whichever fits the box.

## Short (≈40 words) — for tight fields

SYSTEM OVERLOAD are DJ Dieter and Kiki G: one Munich MIDI-workstation salesman who believes he
IS a synthesizer, one Stockholm waitress who can pull a triple espresso on roller skates. Every

## Standard (≈120 words) — Spotify for Artists, Apple Music

SYSTEM OVERLOAD are DJ Dieter and Kiki G, the Eurodance duo that Munich has been unable to
apologise for since 1998.

He was selling MIDI workstations and communicating exclusively via a deep, sleazy whisper. She
was routing a fourteen-person LAN party at Stockholm's premier dial-up cyber café — on roller
skates, holding a tray of lattes, having turned down every producer in Scandinavia. They met in
a sauna. The sauna did not survive.

*Club Humidity — The Moist Mix 2002* is nineteen songs at exactly 138 beats per minute,
recorded at 94% relative humidity, with the engineers in ponchos. Leopard print. Ferraris that
belong to someone else. A dew point, reached repeatedly.

Fifty thousand capacity. Sold out. Stay moist.

## Long (≈220 words) — for a website or press kit

SYSTEM OVERLOAD are DJ Dieter and Kiki G, and they would like you to know that the champagne
is complimentary and the leather is genuine.

Dieter was a MIDI-workstation salesman in Munich, keeping all forty-one framed noise complaints
above the decks and communicating via a deep sleazy whisper and one thin, decisive moustache.
The jacket has never been zipped. The sunglasses have never come off. Not even in the sauna —
especially not in the sauna.

Kiki G was discovered pouring a perfect foam heart, on roller skates, while routing a
fourteen-person LAN party at Stockholm's premier dial-up cyber café. She wears silver because
gold is for people who peaked. She has never once been impressed by Dieter, which is the only
reason this band still exists.

They are accompanied everywhere by three women credited as "backing vocals" who have never been
within four metres of a microphone: Tanja, Mitzi and Brigitte. Doors that were closed are open.
Queues do not apply.

*Club Humidity — The Moist Mix 2002* was mixed entirely at 94% relative humidity. Condensation
ran off the ceiling and rained onto the desk. Two consoles were lost. The masters still smell
faintly of eucalyptus.

Nineteen songs. One tempo. Ibiza, Tokyo, Miami, Berlin. Stay moist.

## Before you paste

- **This is a fictional act and the bio is in character.** That is normal for a comedy or parody
  project and stores accept it, but if a platform asks you to confirm the artist is real, say
  plainly that it is a parody project. Do not assert the backstory as fact to a human reviewer.
- **No real people are referenced.** Keep it that way — an invented Munich salesman is fine;
  a real one is a legal problem.
- **Nothing here claims chart positions, awards or sales.** "Multi-platinum" and "50,000
  capacity" appear on the site as jokes in the artwork, but a bio field is where platforms look
  for factual claims, so they are deliberately absent above. Do not add them back.
